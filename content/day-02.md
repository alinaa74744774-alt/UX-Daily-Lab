# Day 02｜用户画像 User Persona

## 01 Learn｜今日学习

### What is a User Persona?
A **User Persona 用户画像** is a semi-fictional archetype that represents a key segment of your target audience. Rather than describing an actual living person, a persona synthesizes shared behaviors, motivations, goals, and pain points discovered across your user group.
**中文理解：** 用户画像不是某个真实个人的传记，而是从一类用户的共同目标、行为和困扰中提炼出的代表性模型。

In product teams, personas prevent the dangerous trap of **"self-referential design 自我参照设计"**—designing for yourself rather than the real user. They also counter the **"elastic user 弹性用户"** problem, where teams keep redefining the user to justify preferred features. A well-crafted persona gives designers, developers, and product managers a shared reference for deciding whose problems the product should solve.
**中文理解：** 它能帮助团队避免“把自己当用户”，也避免为了支持不同想法而反复改变用户定义；团队可据此聚焦真正服务对象。

> **Core Principle: Focus on Behaviors and Goals, Not Demographic Fluff.**
> Effective personas prioritize **what users are trying to achieve**, **what blocks them**, and **how they make decisions** in context—not invented details such as favorite brands or appearance.
**中文理解：** 画像应说明用户要完成什么、受什么阻碍、如何决策；年龄、外貌等背景只有在影响体验时才值得保留。

## 02 English｜UX English

### 1. Pain Point
**English definition:** A specific, persistent problem or frustration that users encounter when interacting with a product, service, or process.  
**中文解释：** 用户痛点；使用产品或流程中遇到的具体困扰或阻碍。  
**UX-context example:** The persona's primary pain point is the repetitive manual data entry required at checkout.  
**中文辅助理解：** 该用户画像的核心痛点在于结账环节需要反复进行手动数据录入。

### 2. Mental Model
**English definition:** What the user believes or assumes about how a system or product works, formed through past experiences.  
**中文解释：** 心智模型；用户基于既往经验对系统运作逻辑所形成的认知与预期。  
**UX-context example:** Our persona's mental model is anchored in spreadsheet workflows, so navigating a card-based UI feels counterintuitive to them.  
**中文辅助理解：** 该画像的心智模型深受表格工作流的影响，因此卡片式界面的操作方式让他们感觉违背直觉。

### 3. Archetype
**English definition:** An idealized or generalized model representing common behavioral traits and patterns across a broader user segment.  
**中文解释：** 典型原型；代表某一类用户群体共有行为特征的原型模型。  
**UX-context example:** Instead of creating six separate personas, we condensed our findings into one core archetype and one secondary archetype.  
**中文辅助理解：** 我们没有创建六个独立画像，而是将调研提炼为一个核心原型和一个次要原型。

### 4. Behavioral Pattern
**English definition:** A recurring set of actions, habits, or decisions that a user repeatedly exhibits in specific scenarios.  
**中文解释：** 行为模式；用户在特定场景下反复出现的动作习惯或决策倾向。  
**UX-context example:** We observed a consistent behavioral pattern: users frequently bypass onboarding tooltips to explore the interface directly.  
**中文辅助理解：** 我们发现了一种一致的行为模式：用户经常直接跳过新手引导提示，直接探索界面。

### 5. Primary Persona
**English definition:** The main user profile whose needs and goals drive the core feature set and default interaction design of a product.  
**中文解释：** 主画像 / 首要用户画像；产品核心功能与默认交互设计所主要围绕的目标用户。  
**UX-context example:** When feature tradeoffs arise, we prioritize the primary persona over edge-case power users.  
**中文辅助理解：** 当出现功能取舍争议时，我们优先满足主画像的需求，而非边缘极客用户。

---

### Professional UX Expressions｜常用专业表达
- **"Design for the primary, accommodate the secondary."** — Center core decisions on the primary persona while accounting for secondary needs.
- **"Actionable insights over demographic noise."** — Prioritize findings that guide design decisions over irrelevant profile details.

## 03 Challenge

### Scenario
A first-time apartment renter is looking for a compatible roommate.

### Goal
Create a useful proto-persona that helps a product team understand this user's needs and make better design decisions.

### Deliverable
A one-page English proto-persona using the outline in Practice.

### Completion Criteria
1. Define the user's current situation and context in a concise archetype.
2. Include at least two behavior patterns, two goals, and two pain points.
3. Connect each major pain point to a concrete product or interface response.

## 04 Practice｜实践

**Estimated Time:** 40 minutes (30–60 minutes)
**中文辅助：** 建议用 40 分钟完成；可按自己的节奏在 30–60 分钟内调整。

Follow these numbered steps to create your persona artifact:
**中文辅助：** 按以下步骤逐步完成画像作品。

### Step 1: Define the Scenario & Role (8 mins)
Give your persona an archetype label and context line. Avoid fictional storytelling (e.g., "likes long walks on the beach"); define their functional reality.
**中文辅助：** 为画像命名，并用一句话交代用户所处的情境；写真实需求，避免编造与体验无关的故事。
- *Example Label:* "The Cautious Budgeter: Young professional moving to a new city for work."

### Step 2: Map Goals and Motivations (10 mins)
Identify what success looks like from the user's perspective.
**中文辅助：** 描述用户眼中的“成功结果”，而不是先写产品功能。
- *Tip:* Ask: "What job is this user hiring our app to do?"
- Formulate 2–3 actionable statements (e.g., "Find a roommate with compatible sleep schedules within 3 weeks").
**中文辅助：** 写 2–3 条可观察、可行动的目标，例如在三周内找到作息相近的室友。

### Step 3: Identify Friction and Pain Points (10 mins)
List the specific obstacles, fears, or manual burdens they encounter.
**中文辅助：** 找出用户在过程中遇到的具体阻碍、担忧或繁琐操作。
- Focus on emotional and functional barriers (e.g., "Fear of scams or unvetted strangers," "Fatigue from endless messaging without responses").
**中文辅助：** 同时考虑情绪和实际操作层面的阻碍，例如担心诈骗，或因消息石沉大海而疲惫。

### Step 4: Translate into UX Implications (12 mins)
Connect user needs directly to design requirements. For every pain point, propose a concrete product response.
**中文辅助：** 把用户需求转化成设计要求；每个重要痛点都要对应一个具体产品回应。
- *Formula:* "Because [Persona] struggles with [Pain Point], the UI must [Specific Feature/Design Choice]."
**中文辅助：** 可套用表达：“由于该画像在［痛点］上遇到困难，界面需要［具体功能或设计选择］。”

---

### Deliverable Content Outline｜交付物输出结构
```markdown
# Persona: [Archetype Name / Title]
- Context & Scenario: [1-2 sentences on their current situation]

## 1. Behaviors & Mental Model
- [Observed behavior or expectation 1]
- [Observed behavior or expectation 2]

## 2. Core Goals (What they want to achieve)
- [Goal 1]
- [Goal 2]

## 3. Key Pain Points (What blocks them)
- [Pain Point 1]
- [Pain Point 2]

## 4. Design Takeaways (UX Implications)
- [Feature / UI suggestion solving Pain Point 1]
- [Feature / UI suggestion solving Pain Point 2]
```

**中文辅助理解：**
- Context & Scenario：用 1–2 句话交代用户所处环境及当前任务。
- Behaviors & Mental Model：写用户反复出现的行为，以及他们认为产品如何运作。
- Core Goals：描述用户希望达成的结果。
- Key Pain Points：列出妨碍目标实现的具体困难。
- Design Takeaways：将发现转化为可执行的界面或功能建议。

## 05 Review｜学习检查

### Self-Checklist
- [ ] Does your persona focus on behaviors, goals, and frustrations rather than superficial demographic details?  
      *(画像是否聚焦于行为、目标与困扰，而非无关的个人背景细节？)*
- [ ] Are the goals written as user outcomes rather than system features?  
      *(目标是否以“用户的预期成果”书写，而非写成“系统功能”？)*
- [ ] Can a UI designer read your "Design Takeaways" and immediately know what feature or safety net to build?  
      *(界面设计师看到你的“设计启示”后，能否立即明白该做什么界面或防护设计？)*

### Reflection Question
> *When a stakeholder suggests adding a feature that doesn't fit the primary persona, what professional English phrasing would you use to steer the conversation back to the persona's core needs?*  
> *(当业务方提出一个明显偏离主画像需求的功能时，你会如何用专业英文沟通，引导团队重新聚焦主画像的核心诉求？)*
