FOUNDATION 01

User Research

Research replaces assumptions.

Understand people’s goals, context, and behavior before deciding what to build.

UX 研究要先了解用户想完成什么、身处什么情境、实际如何行动，再判断设计机会；不能把设计者的经验直接当成用户需求。

WHY
02 · METHOD

Semi-structured Interview

Use a prepared guide of open questions, then follow relevant details in the participant’s story.

用开放式核心问题保持研究聚焦，再根据参与者的真实经历灵活追问。

HOW TO EXPLORE
HOW
03 · PRINCIPLE

Evidence over Assumptions

Ground conclusions in observed behavior, interview evidence, and context. Treat expectations as hypotheses.

判断应优先依据用户的真实行为、访谈证据和具体情境，而不是凭设计者的猜测推断需求；用户表达的期待仍需结合行为验证。
